endomorphism ring

Each row pairs an isomorphism class of CM lattices with an isomorphism class of elliptic curves over Fp; the two sides are matched by an equivalence of categories. lattice (a, b, c) is the reduced binary quadratic form ax2 + bxy + cy2 pinning down the lattice class, and curve (f, g) the short Weierstrass model y2 = x3 + fx + g. Rows sharing a lattice class are quadratic twists of one another.
